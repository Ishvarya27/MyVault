Your data

Everything you enter is stored privately on this device (in this browser's local database). Nothing is sent anywhere. Export a backup file any time — you can re-import it later or move it to another device.

Install as an app

On Android Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen. On iPhone Safari: tap the Share icon → Add to Home Screen. MyVault will then open full-screen like a regular app, with your data already saved.
